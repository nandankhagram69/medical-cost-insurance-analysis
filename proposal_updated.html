Project Proposal: Medical Cost Personal (Insurance) Data Analysis

1. Project Definition

This project aims to analyze medical insurance charges using Python. It will explore how age, sex, BMI, number of children, smoking status, and region are associated with insurance costs. The project will include comprehensive data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, at least six statistical visualizations, an interactive User Interface (UI) dashboard with cost prediction, and a short insights report.

2. Dataset Use Case

Dataset: Medical Cost Personal Datasets
Source: Kaggle
Link: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mirichoi0218/insurance

The dataset contains 1,338 records and seven columns: age, sex, BMI, children, smoker, region, and charges. It will be used to explore patterns in insurance costs and compare charges across different groups.

3. Planned Visualizations and Expected Outcomes

Graph/Plot Expected Outcome
Histogram of insurance charges Understand the distribution and common ranges of charges.
Box plot: smoker vs. non-smoker Compare typical charges and variability by smoking status.
Scatter plot: age vs. charges Examine the relationship between age and insurance costs.
Scatter plot: BMI vs. charges Identify patterns between BMI and insurance costs.
Bar chart: average charges by region Compare average charges across regions.
Bar chart: average charges by sex Compare average charges between males and females.

Actual findings will be reported after analyzing the dataset.
